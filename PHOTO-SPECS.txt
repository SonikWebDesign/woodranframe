WOODRA A-FRAME · PHOTO DELIVERY SPEC

Ask the photographer to provide the ORIGINAL exported files, not WhatsApp/Messenger copies.

COLOR / FORMAT
- JPEG (.jpg), quality 90–95%, sRGB color profile.
- No HEIC for the final delivery.
- No text, logos or watermarks burned into the image.
- Keep a natural edit; avoid crushed blacks and very heavy filters.
- We can make WebP/AVIF web versions later. The photographer should give us the high-quality masters.

RESOLUTION
- Keep full-resolution originals, ideally 4000–6000 px on the long edge.
- Do not deliver anything important below 2400 px on the long edge.

HERO / WIDE WEBSITE PHOTOS
- Landscape 16:9 or 3:2.
- Minimum: 2560×1440 px.
- Ideal website master: 3200×1800 px or 3600×2025 px.
- For each key scene, also shoot a vertical version for phones.

MOBILE / VERTICAL COMPANION PHOTOS
- 4:5 or 3:4.
- Minimum: 1600×2000 px.
- Ideal: 2160×2700 px or larger.

GALLERY / INTERIOR / DETAILS
- Landscape 3:2 or 4:3, long edge 3000–4500 px.
- Portrait details 4:5, around 2400×3000 px or larger.

VERY IMPORTANT FOR CROPPING
For the 4–6 strongest scenes, shoot BOTH landscape and portrait versions:
1. Both A-frame houses together
2. Sunset exterior
3. Pool + houses
4. Mountain / terrace view
5. Night exterior / stars
6. Breakfast or lifestyle terrace scene

Keep the main subject within the central ~65% of the frame and leave a little breathing room around roofs, people and furniture. This lets us crop for desktop, tablet and mobile without cutting the important part.

DELIVERY
- Originals via Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer, not chat apps.
- Keep EXIF/orientation correct.
- Do not pre-crop all images to one ratio. Give us the landscape and portrait selects separately.
